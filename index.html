WE ARE HERE Company

Hi fi dance in lo fi ways.

The WE ARE HERE Company is a professional development platform and dynamic community of powerful performers who are keen to research, process, make, discuss, disrupt and perform new kinds of dance work made in collaboration with and led by Emma Saunders.

We are currently hosted by Critical Path, Sydney NSW.
